Assume a river that flows from top to bottom and there are a number of docks along the way $(1\cdots n)$ that we pass along the stream. We take a raft down stream and we can stop at any dock. We have to pay a ticket:

$t_{i,j} = \text{cost of going from i to j \qquad (i < j)}$

(i is lower than j because we are going downstream)

These costs are completely general and arbitrary. Our goal is to travel from dock 1 to dock n at the minimum cost.
